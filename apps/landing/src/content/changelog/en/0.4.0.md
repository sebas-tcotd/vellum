Vellum learns Spanish, lets you pick a theme and opens your `.cslmap` with a double click on Windows.

## New

- **A theme selector.**
- **The interface in English and Spanish.**
- **On Windows, `.cslmap` files open in Vellum.**
- **Export notices** close by themselves or with a click.
- **A new logo** and refreshed Day colours.

## Improved

- Big cities draw faster: the heaviest map sources are capped and the map uses more workers.

## Fixed

- Buildings of the DLCs get their real service type.
- A layer that fails no longer stops the rest of the map from drawing.
- **0.4.1** · Blimp routes get their style back, and airplanes are recognised too.
