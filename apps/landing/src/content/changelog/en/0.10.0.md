Your transit network as a metro diagram, in a view of its own next to the geographic map.

## New

- **A schematic view**, separate from the map, drawn with the rules of LOOM by Bast and Brosi.
- **Octilinear and orthoradial layouts** to compare the same network two ways.
- **Read and control the network:** highlight a line and find every line in order in the sidebar.
- **Line labels**, an orthoradial grid and a manual relayout.
- **Semantic zoom** that groups nearby stations as you pull out.
