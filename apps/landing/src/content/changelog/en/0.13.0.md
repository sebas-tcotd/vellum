A big release for transit: the schematic view is reorganised station by station, and forests gain two new ways to look.

## New

- **Forests as vegetation circles or as a heat map**, besides the tree crowns.
- **Roads by category:** show or hide rail, air and water routes separately.
- **Surface trains appear on the minimap.**
- **Parklife tours** are recognised as transit modes of their own.
- **Bridge names the stops of stations with a building** and saves each city in its own folder, stamped with your local time.

## Improved

- In the schematic view, each station is a single node and rails take priority.
- Schematic lines follow a cleaner grid and turn at stations.
- The schematic camera moves like the one of the map, and smoothly.

## Fixed

- The stop card opens on the left and no longer covers the camera controls.
- At close zoom, stations are the same size as their lines.
- **0.13.1** · On Linux, the window groups with its icon in the panel.
- **0.13.2** · Parks keep their real type from the game; the place card shows a single list of specialisations and a title for districts without a name; the cable car comes back with the roads layer.
