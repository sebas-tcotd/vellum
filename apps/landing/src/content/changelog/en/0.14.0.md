The Day theme is reordered so roads read by importance at a glance, and the coast gets an ink line of its own.

## New

- **Day with a road hierarchy.** Motorways, avenues and streets stand apart better, and the city takes on a clay-pink tone.
- **Ink shoreline:** the coast is drawn in its own colour, derived from the water of the theme.
- **A new Windows icon** in the Fluent style of the system, in the taskbar too.

## Improved

- Roads in tunnels look continuous and dimmed instead of cut off.
- The built-in themes always appear in the same order, on every system.
- The geographic map no longer shows transfer markers, which cluttered it.

## Fixed

- Inland water and the coast no longer drift apart: they come from the same geometry.
- The minimap and the pan limit frame the whole world.
- The About window names the game, and its Close button no longer vanishes on hover.
