---

Exports gain real map marginalia, the `.vellumstyle` format becomes public, and Windows gets a recommended installer.

## New

- **Marginalia in PNG and SVG exports:** the city name, legends, scale and north, like on a printed map.
- **The `.vellumstyle` format is public**, with its JSON schema, and your themes stay compatible as Vellum evolves.
- **Transfers between transit modes** are drawn on the map once they are confirmed.
- **On Windows, the `.exe` installer becomes the recommended one**, with a splash screen while it installs.

## Fixed

- The export captures the whole base composition, and the PNG waits until everything is drawn.
- Native radio buttons and checkboxes follow the dark theme.
- Silent installs on Windows skip the language dialog.
- The map engine moves to MapLibre 6, with its security fixes.
