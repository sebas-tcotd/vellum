The first public versions of Vellum: open the `.cslmap` of CSL Map View and see your city as a map.

## New

- **Open `.cslmap` files** exported with CSL Map View.
- **Layers:** terrain with elevation colours and relief, roads, buildings, forests, districts, parks and transit with each of its modes.
- **Turn layers on and off with 1…7** and open their options with Shift + 1…7.
- **Export to PNG and SVG.**
- **Five built-in themes**, and your own themes as `.vellumstyle` files.
- **Rotation, clean mode and a minimap.**

## Fixed

- **0.2.1–0.2.8** · Releases and the update manifest are published more reliably, so updates find the right files.
