Vellum is no longer an early version. It arrives in the Microsoft Store, and Vellum Bridge gets its own options panel and tells you where it left your city. Thanks to everyone who tried the 0.x versions and said what was missing.

## New

- **Vellum in the Microsoft Store.** Install it from the Store: it comes signed, without the SmartScreen warning, and updates itself.
- **Vellum Bridge has its own options panel**, in English and Spanish.
- **Bridge tells you where it saved your city** when the export is done.

## Improved

- The welcome screen is simpler, and its buttons show that they heard your click.

## Fixed

- Maps opened from a Bridge export keep their real file name in the marginalia.
- Heating pipes from Snowfall no longer draw as streets across the whole map.
