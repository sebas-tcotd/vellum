Vellum dresses its window in the style of each system: Mica on Windows, Liquid Glass on macOS.

## New

- **Window materials in the style of Mica and Liquid Glass**, with a sidebar in the style of Liquid Glass on macOS.
- **The sidebar adapts to the window** and you can resize it.
- **A dark theme for the app chrome.**
- **The native menu is reorganised** into submenus and follows your language.
- **Pending updates install** from a command of the app.
