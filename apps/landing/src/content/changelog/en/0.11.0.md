Vellum opens the cities that Vellum Bridge exports from the game, and with them come street names, a place card and districts coloured by what they do.

## New

- **Open a city exported with Vellum Bridge** (`.vellummap`) right in Vellum.
- **Street names and the real width of each road** at detail zoom.
- **A place card:** select a district, a park or a building to see what it is.
- **Districts coloured by specialisation**, named by default, with an optional marker.
- **Forests as tree crowns** instead of one circle per cell.
- **A keyboard shortcuts sheet**, opened with `?`.
- **Precise zoom** from the camera controls, and the map settles back on the city when you pull out past it.
- **Shift + Click** on a layer of the rail opens its options.

## Improved

- In the schematic view, stop names run across the line and keep clear of each other.
- The loading screen stays until the map is really drawn.
- Cities open faster: the file is read and the terrain is built in parallel.
- The forest green of Day is easier to read.

## Fixed

- Water is classified by its depth, not against the sea level.
- At junctions, the minor street ends under the major road.
- The SVG export follows the real width of the roads, like the map.
- Shift + 1…7 opens the layer options from any row of the keyboard.
- The welcome screen no longer promises a future exporter.
- **0.11.1** · Tree crowns keep loading after panning at detail zoom, and going back to the whole city is one smooth move.
