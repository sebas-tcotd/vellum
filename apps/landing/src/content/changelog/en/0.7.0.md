Vellum locks down its desktop app and gives a few unusual roads a style of their own.

## New

- **A desktop app locked down for security and privacy**, with guarded contracts between the map and the app.
- **Pedestrian streets** get their own style, and helicopter routes no longer show up as roads.
- **The cable car** gets its own treatment.
- **The sidebar gradient** follows the active theme.

## Fixed

- Dashed lines come out at the right size in exports.
- Roads are classified with the right vocabulary from the game.
- Colour mixes and opacity of the themes in the style of Mica are adjusted.
